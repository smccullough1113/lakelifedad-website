How estimates work

Fuel use is estimated from trip distance and observed fuel economy. In gallons-per-hour mode, hourly burn is converted to gallons per mile using your average riding speed. Your target includes the fuel for the trip and your reserve preference; “Plan fuel to bring” adds one-third of trip fuel for emergencies. The result compares that target with the fuel currently onboard.
